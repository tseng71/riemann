# Prior-art check — 2026-08-12

This is a reproducibility note for reviewers, not a priority opinion.  It
records the primary sources checked while preparing the public draft:

- Anthropic, [`zeta-23-lean`](https://github.com/anthropics/zeta-23-lean),
  including the multiplicity-aware Theorem D implementation;
- Goldston, Lee, Schettler, and Suriajaya,
  [arXiv:2503.15449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15449);
- Goldston and Suriajaya,
  [arXiv:2511.20059](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20059);
- Goldston and Suriajaya,
  [arXiv:2603.28104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28104);
- the earlier public verifier
  [`ainta/zeta-simple-zeros`](https://github.com/ainta/zeta-simple-zeros);
- the clean-room refinement
  [`learademacher/ai-refines-ai-zeta-bound`](https://github.com/learademacher/ai-refines-ai-zeta-bound).

The three arXiv records match the titles, author lists, and identifiers used
in the manuscript.  The two verifier repositories publicly predate this
release and are credited in `verifier/PROVENANCE.md`.

No database search can establish mathematical novelty or priority.  Before
journal submission, a subject-matter expert should repeat the search across
MathSciNet, zbMATH, arXiv, and citation chains and should compare the exact
stability-defect and seven-window construction, not merely numerical bounds.
